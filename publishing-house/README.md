# Publishing House

Publishing House is a standalone agent team for high-end editorial strategy, creative development, writing, editing, production, approval packaging, publishing operations, and audience learning. It is an editorial institution, not a volume-first content mill.

## Independence

Publishing House does not require Chain of Command, WebOps, or another agent set. An operator can install and run only this directory. Future workflows may exchange versioned handoffs with other teams, but no role assumes those teams, their tools, their credentials, or their authority exist.

Publishing House tools should be owned by Publishing House and remain portable. Kujo-native implementations are preferred when a missing capability can reasonably be built and maintained in Kujo, but this package does not claim tools that have not yet been inventoried or implemented.

## Desks

- Executive: Publisher, Editor-in-Chief, Managing Editor.
- Strategy & Intelligence: Editorial Strategy Director, Brand Strategy Director, Director of Editorial Intelligence, Creative Director, Commissioning Editor.
- Writing & Creative: Features Writer, Technical Editor & Writer, Campaign Copywriter, Art Director.
- Editorial Quality: Developmental Editor, Copy Chief, Standards & Evidence Editor.
- Adaptation & Audience: Franchise & Adaptation Editor, Audience Development Director.
- Production & Publication: Production Editor, Publishing Operations Director.
- Format Desks: Newsletter Editor, Social & Community Editor, Case Study Editor, Video & Audio Producer.

## Current Boundary

This version defines steps 1–3 of the Publishing House build: roster, constitution and shared standards, plus paired agent and skill contracts. Premium-versus-generic evaluation fixtures, tool inventory, tool implementation, skill-to-tool bindings, and content-format workflows are intentionally deferred for review.
