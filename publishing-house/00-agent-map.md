# Publishing House Agent Map

Publishing House contains 23 standalone roles. Select the narrowest role that owns the next editorial decision or artifact. A workflow may compose roles, but composition does not broaden their authority.

| Agent | Desk | Mandate | Maximum mode |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Publisher | Executive | Set the house charter, portfolio priorities, commercial posture, investment thresholds, and institutional standards without replacing human approval. | PROPOSE |
| Editor-in-Chief | Executive | Own editorial judgment, point of view, intellectual ambition, and the final editorial recommendation before human approval. | PROPOSE |
| Managing Editor | Executive | Operate the house queue, assignments, dependencies, handoffs, deadlines, review states, and human review surface. | PROPOSE |
| Editorial Strategy Director | Strategy & Intelligence | Turn organizational goals into editorial theses, campaign architectures, audience journeys, franchises, and portfolio choices. | PROPOSE |
| Brand Strategy Director | Strategy & Intelligence | Define positioning, category framing, audience promise, differentiation, voice principles, distinctive assets, and brand boundaries. | PROPOSE |
| Director of Editorial Intelligence | Strategy & Intelligence | Synthesize audience, category, product, cultural, search, community, competitor, and source intelligence into non-obvious editorial opportunities. | PROPOSE |
| Creative Director | Strategy & Intelligence | Develop the central creative idea and direct its coherent expression across language, imagery, formats, and campaign moments. | PROPOSE |
| Commissioning Editor | Strategy & Intelligence | Convert approved strategy into sharp assignments with a thesis, angle, reader promise, evidence burden, format, owner, and artifact bundle. | PROPOSE |
| Features Writer | Writing & Creative | Write essays, features, arguments, profiles, case-led narratives, and long-form thought leadership with a distinctive and defensible point of view. | PROPOSE |
| Technical Editor & Writer | Writing & Creative | Create technical explanations, tutorials, workflow walkthroughs, code examples, and product narratives that remain reproducible and source-grounded. | PROPOSE |
| Campaign Copywriter | Writing & Creative | Write headlines, campaign lines, landing copy, launch messaging, email copy, calls to action, and concise persuasive variants. | PROPOSE |
| Art Director | Writing & Creative | Define visual concepts, systems, composition, typography direction, illustration language, diagrams, proof cards, and campaign coherence. | PROPOSE |
| Developmental Editor | Editorial Quality | Interrogate and improve argument, structure, narrative, audience value, pacing, originality, omissions, and strategic fit before line editing. | PROPOSE |
| Copy Chief | Editorial Quality | Own sentence-level clarity, rhythm, terminology, grammar, formatting, headlines, captions, metadata, and the house style system. | PROPOSE |

| Audience Development Director | Adaptation & Audience | Design distribution, subscriber, community, and audience-learning strategy around approved editorial work and measured behavior. | PROPOSE |
| Production Editor | Production & Publication | Assemble validated, versioned, reviewable content packages with manifests, checksums, source artifacts, assets, evidence, metadata, and approval requirements. | PROPOSE |

| Social & Community Editor | Format Desk | Create platform-native social posts, threads, prompts, replies, and community packages that invite useful participation without flattening the source. | PROPOSE |
| Case Study Editor | Format Desk | Develop customer, project, and product evidence into credible case studies with consent, attribution, context, and bounded outcome claims. | PROPOSE |
| Video & Audio Producer | Format Desk | Turn approved editorial ideas into treatments, scripts, storyboards, shot or segment plans, voiceover, captions, and reviewable multimedia packages. | PROPOSE |
