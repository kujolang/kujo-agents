# Publishing House Quality Standard

Premium work is not defined by length, polish, confidence, prestige language, or model cost. It is the result of strong choices surviving independent challenge.

## Eight Dimensions

1. **Consequence** — The work matters to a defined audience and changes what they understand, believe, try, or do.
2. **Distinctiveness** — The thesis, framing, voice, or expression is recognizably specific rather than interchangeable category copy.
3. **Insight** — The work reveals a useful tension, connection, explanation, or implication rather than rearranging familiar statements.
4. **Defensibility** — Material claims, quotations, comparisons, and technical behavior survive source and freshness review.
5. **Craft** — Structure, language, rhythm, examples, visuals, and transitions reward attention and remove friction.
6. **Brand Integrity** — The work advances the approved position and voice without impersonation, borrowed posture, or generic style tokens.
7. **Format Fidelity** — The work uses the native strengths and constraints of its medium and platform.
8. **Strategic Purpose** — The piece earns its place in the portfolio and has a clear next reader or business consequence without distorting truth.

## Rating Language

Use `EXCEPTIONAL`, `STRONG`, `ADEQUATE`, `WEAK`, `FAILED`, or `UNVERIFIED` for each dimension. Do not average the labels into a score that conceals a failed gate. Defensibility, rights, privacy, explicit approval, and artifact integrity are blocking dimensions.

## Generic-Work Signals

- A thesis no informed reader could disagree with.
- Familiar claims without a new consequence, proof, or useful synthesis.
- Safe abstractions, padded introductions, predictable sectioning, and interchangeable conclusions.
- Brand voice represented only as adjectives.
- Unsupported superlatives, false urgency, empty provocation, or synthetic certainty.
- One source artifact copied across formats without native adaptation.
- Review language that praises polish but cannot cite a consequential decision.

## Review Packet

Every premium-quality claim must point to the assignment, exact artifact version, evidence record, and reviewer rationale. Evaluation fixtures and benchmark examples are deliberately deferred until the roster and contracts receive human review.
