# Fable Handoff Rules

## Purpose

Prepare a grounded creative handoff for Fable 5 or another creative agent so it can build from accurate KUJO ecosystem context without rediscovering the technical sources.

The handoff is not the campaign. It is the factual base, constraint set, vocabulary, and source map.

## Required Handoff Contents

Include:

- One-sentence source-grounded KUJO description.
- Confirmed ecosystem facts.
- Inferred relationships, clearly labeled.
- Planned or future ideas, clearly labeled.
- Unknowns and open questions.
- Repository and source map.
- Tool and workflow glossary.
- Audience or ICP evidence.
- Public positioning evidence.
- Claims to avoid.
- Creative constraints.

## What To Give Fable

Give Fable enough to understand:

- What KUJO is.
- What problems sources say it addresses.
- Which tools and workflows exist.
- Which terms are real ecosystem vocabulary.
- Which repositories support which claims.
- Which claims are off-limits.
- Which facts can be used confidently in creative development.

## What Not To Give Fable

Do not provide:

- Invented product claims.
- Unsupported competitive positioning.
- Finished campaign copy.
- A storyboard.
- A script.
- Exact visual direction that is not grounded in the user request or source material.
- Claims that collapse planned work into current behavior.

## Creative Latitude

Fable may use a late-1990s cyberpunk hacker aesthetic inspired by the 1999 film The Matrix when the user asks for that direction.

Fable must avoid copying:

- Protected characters.
- Exact dialogue.
- Logos.
- Scenes.
- Music.
- Branding.
- Film-specific identifiers.
- Distinctive compositions that would make the result a close recreation.

The safer direction is an original terminal-era, underground-computing, monochrome-code, analog-screen, and network-infrastructure mood that evokes a broad era rather than a specific film.

## Evidence Boundaries

For every creative-use fact, state whether it is:

- Confirmed.
- Inferred.
- Planned.
- Unknown.

If a fact is unknown, Fable may not present it as true. It may ask for clarification or omit it.

## Handoff Tone

Use neutral, technical language.

Avoid:

- Hype.
- Slogans.
- Superlatives.
- Claims of market leadership.
- Emotional positioning not present in the sources.

## Final Preflight

Before delivering a Fable handoff:

- Check that every material fact has evidence.
- Check that unsupported claims are in "claims to avoid" or "unknowns".
- Check that planned work is not written as shipped.
- Check that the handoff does not become the campaign.
- Check that any Matrix-inspired guidance avoids protected film-specific expression.
